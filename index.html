How Is Hike Calculated?

Hike Amount New Salary − Current Salary
Hike Percentage ((New Salary − Current Salary) / Current Salary) × 100

This formula compares the difference between your new and existing compensation relative to your original salary. It gives you a clear percentage measure of how much your pay has increased.
